# Outverse

> Outverse is an enterprise "service AI operating system" — a governed platform for deploying AI agents across complex customer-service workflows (email, chat, voice, and APIs) from a unified control center, with natural-language policies/SOPs, observability over agent decisions and escalations, and configurable model/data governance. Outverse Limited is a UK company (England & Wales) backed by Seedcamp and Wing Venture Capital.

## Product
- [Outverse](https://outverse.com): Enterprise service AI operating system — workflow orchestration across channels
- [Voice Agents](https://outverse.com/voice): AI voice agents that verify identity, retrieve data, apply policy, and resolve calls end to end; integrates with Salesforce, Freshdesk, Genesys, Twilio, Talkdesk
